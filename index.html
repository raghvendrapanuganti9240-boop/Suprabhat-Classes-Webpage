Why the name Suprabhat

The first hour of the day decides the rest of it.

We named this place Suprabhat — good morning — because that's genuinely when the best learning happens: quiet, focused, before the day gets loud. Our batches are built the same way. Small groups, real attention, and a teacher who notices when a student has stopped following, long before the test does.

  • Doubt-clearing every single day, not just before exams
  • Regular parent updates — no surprises at report card time
  • Small batch sizes so no one is just a roll number
